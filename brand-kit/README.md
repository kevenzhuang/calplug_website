# CalPlug brand kit

For posters, flyers, slides and anything else CalPlug shows to the world. It matches the website, so a workshop flyer, a talk and calplug.org look like they come from the same place. The rules behind it are in `DESIGN.md` (sections 3, 6 and 10).

## What is here

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `calplug-slides-template.pptx` | Slides (16:9): cover, section divider, content. Start every CalPlug talk from it |
| `calplug-flyer-template.pptx` | Event flyer, US Letter portrait. Replace the text and put one photo or diagram in the gray box |
| `logos/calplug-logo.svg` | Full logo as a vector (traced; scales to any size). Use this one |

| `logos/calplug-logo-compact.png` | Icon and CALPLUG wordmark only, for small spaces |

## The rules in one minute

**Colors**

| Color | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| CalPlug blue | `#005BAA` | Highlights, links, the top half of the ring |
| CalPlug green | `#8BC53F` | Shapes and lines only, never text |
| Dark green | `#4A7A1C` | Small labels such as "CALPLUG WORKSHOP", dates |
| Navy | `#0E2A47` | Bottom band, section slides, titles |
| Ink | `#1B2530` | Body text |
| Gray | `#5B6875` | Small print |

**Type.** Titles in Montserrat (bold), text in Inter. If a computer does not have them, Arial is the fallback (UC Irvine's own web font). Slides: titles 30 to 40 pt, text at least 18 pt.

**Logos.** The CalPlug logo goes top left. The UC Irvine logo and the Calit2 logo sit in the navy band at the bottom, apart from each other, never joined. Do not redraw, recolor, stretch or crop any logo.

**The ring.** The half-blue, half-green ring from the logo is the only decoration. Use it once per page or slide, large and partly off the edge, or small around an icon. Do not add other shapes, gradients or clip art.

**Photos.** Real people at work in the lab, at events, with equipment. No stock photos.
