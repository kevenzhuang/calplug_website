The One Kilowatt-hour Challenge was a community outreach program that was designed as an Educational Program where community members within UCI could learn exactly how much energy a Kilowatt-hour is. With Covid restrictions in place the 1 Kilowatt-hour Challenges are on hold.

The 1 kWh Challenge

Why

Designed to be accessible to the community, the 1 Kilowatt Challenge’s purpose was quantifying and defining what a kWh was in actual usage.
