The One Kilowatt-hour Challenge was a community outreach program that was designed as an educational program where community members within UCI could learn exactly how much energy a kilowatt-hour is. The challenges are currently on hold.

Why

Designed to be accessible to the community, the 1 kWh Challenge’s purpose was to quantify and define what a kWh is in actual usage.
