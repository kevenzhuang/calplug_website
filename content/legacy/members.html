Dear Members,

Thank you for your interest in supporting CalPlug.

CalPlug is guided by members who support our community of scholars, researchers, and staff. Their membership fees support the vision we share of doing our part to save the planet through energy efficiency, greenhouse gas reduction one Watt at a time, and the design of plug-load devices. 

CalPlug researches a wide variety of projects with the goal to deliver solutions focused on energy efficiency, evaluations of consumer electronics, standards development and policy, education, public outreach, and user behavior studies.

Join CalPlug today to contribute to endeavors that make a difference today and in the future!
