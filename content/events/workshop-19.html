CalPlug’s 19th workshop and 10th anniversary celebration was held in Calit2’s own auditorium. Discussion during the event centered around climate resiliency, lowering greenhouse gas emissions, and inclusivity.

Speakers: G.P. Li, Calit2 and CalPlug Research Center; Doug Johnson, Consumer Technology Association; Kim Trenbath, National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL); Benjamin Park, Enevate; Matthias Kurwing, Enervee; Amy Lebar, National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL); Ammi Amarnath, Electric Power Research Institute (EPRI); John Woolsey, Center for Sustainable Energy; Li Zhao, CalPlug Research Center; Jay Seidel, Fullerton Drone Lab
