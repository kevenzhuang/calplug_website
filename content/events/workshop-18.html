CalPlug’s 18th workshop was hosted as a webinar, and discussed the processes of optimizing energy efficiency in spaces, in commercial offices and in the home.

Speakers: G.P. Li, Calit2/CalPlug Research Center; Li Zhao, CalPlug Research Center; John Anderson, OhmConnect; Mary Ann Piette, Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory; Paul Campbell, Facil AI; Michael Myer, Pacific Northwest National Laboratory; Kurt Gokbudak, Schneider Electric

Agenda

  • 9:00–9:05 am | G.P. Li, Calit2/California Plug Load Research Center: Welcome & Introduction
  • 9:05–9:20 am | Li Zhao, CalPlug: Challenges and Opportunities in Energy Transition, Decarbonization and Electrification: CalPlug’s Holistic Approach
  • 9:20–9:45 am | John Anderson, OhmConnect: WiFi smart plugs: the ‘gateway drug’ to home energy automation
  • 9:45–10:10 am | Mary Ann Piette, Lawrence Berkeley Laboratory: Overview of the California Load Flexibility Research and Deployment Hub
  • 10:10–10:35 am | Paul Campbell, Facil AI: Object Oriented Design in Energy and Decision Management
  • 10:35 am | Break
  • 10:40–11:05 am | Michael Myer, Pacific Northwest National Laboratory: Recent field experience and lessons learned related to automatic receptacle controls
  • 11:05–11:30 am | Kurt Gokbudak, Schneider Electric: Buildings of the Future, Today. Planning for Sustainability and Resilience working towards a Healthy Building Environment
  • 11:30 am | Discussion
  • 12:00–12:05 pm | Wrap-up: G.P. Li
